Privacy policy

Applies to Warden Station and Warden Lightkeeper. Last updated 20 October 2026.

RT Sullivan Consulting Limited (“we”) makes these games. We do not collect, store or share any personal data from them.

What stays on your device

To adapt to how you play, the games record gameplay events (for example hits taken, aim accuracy, time spent in a room, puzzle results and choices) and train small models from them. This data and the models are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Speech and any rewording of in-game lines by Apple Intelligence also happen on your device.

You can delete what a game has learned at any time: in Warden Station, open Settings, then WARDEN’s Log, and tap Wipe WARDEN’s notes. Deleting a game removes all of its data.

What we do not do

The games have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. They do not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The games do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@sullivanltd.co.uk. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
