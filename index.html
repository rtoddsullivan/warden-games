Warden games

Games for iPhone and iPad by RT Sullivan Consulting Limited. Each one adapts to how you play, and all of that learning happens on your device.

Warden Station

A top-down roguelite shooter on Halcyon Starport, where the station AI, WARDEN, studies how you play and keeps notes across every death.

Warden Lightkeeper

A cosy, eerie sci-fi story game. You are the new Keeper of Vigil-9, a beacon at the edge of a dark nebula, with ECHO, an AI companion missing much of its memory.
