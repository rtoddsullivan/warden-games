Warden Station support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email station@sullivanltd.co.uk and tell us your device and iOS version. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Common questions

How does the game adapt? WARDEN, the station AI, watches how you play: how often you get hit, how you aim, how quickly you clear rooms. Between rooms it adjusts things like enemy numbers, warning times, deck layouts and how your Medic drone behaves, and its captions tell you what it changed. Bosses never change mid-fight.

Does any of that leave my device? No. Play recording, the engagement model and its personalised training all run on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I start fresh? Open Settings, then WARDEN’s Log, and tap Wipe WARDEN’s notes. That resets everything WARDEN has learned about you.

The game is too hard. The gear menu has assists you control: aim assist, auto-fire (one-thumb play), story mode and bigger touch controls.

Can I use a controller? Yes, any controller supported by iOS: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires, R1 or B dashes.

What is free? The first deck, the Breacher and the Medic drone are free. A one-time unlock opens the rest of the station. What you paid never changes how WARDEN treats you, and nothing that adds power is for sale.

Restoring a purchase: use Restore Purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple Account.
